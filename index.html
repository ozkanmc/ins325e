Course Overview

What will you learn?

Railway Engineering (INS 325E) teaches you to design a complete railway line from scratch. You will define the geometry of curves, calculate gradients, understand how vehicles behave on track, analyse capacity, and ultimately submit a full Railway Plan & Profile as your semester project.

Every formula you learn is used by engineers at TCDD, DB, SNCF, and Network Rail right now — on lines carrying millions of passengers.

Course objective: By the end, you can evaluate, analyse, calculate, and draw a complete railway plan and profile to professional standard.
EVALUATEthe role of railways in multimodal transport and their environmental advantage
ANALYSEgeometric standards — gauge, clearance, curves, gradients, superelevation, transition curves
CALCULATEvehicle dynamics, traction, braking, resistance forces and energy consumption
DESIGNa complete railway route plan and vertical profile to TCDD standards
EXPLAINrailway capacity principles, track structure, and the role of signalling
